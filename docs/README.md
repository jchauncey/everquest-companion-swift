## The screens

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Overview](overview.md) | Statistics about all of your play: loot, sales, kills, motes, leveling, DPS. |
| [Combat](combat.md) | Every fight, one mob at a time: your damage by ability and class, your pet, what the kill paid. |
| [Leveling](leveling.md) | Level and AA progress, your pace, the best spells for your level, your AA ladders. |
| [Motes](motes.md) | Which mobs and camps give you motes, and how often. |
| [Gear](gear.md) | Item search and upgrade planning, exaltations, your character sheet and wish list. |

The other tabs:

- **Loot** — everything you have looted, by item or line by line, with where it came from.
- **Maps** — the zone you are in, its mobs placed where the map data knows them. Paste a `/loc`
  line into the toolbar to mark where you are.
- **Raid Targets** — the raid bosses, what they drop, and your kills of them.
- **Plane of Sky** — the 95 class tests: what you hold toward each, what is ready to hand in, who to
  pull next, and how far each class is from its unlock.
- **Alerts** — spoken and on-screen alerts for lines in your log (a tell, a mob's emote, a buff
  wearing off), with voice packs.
- **Timers** — respawn clocks for the mobs you kill.
- **Events, Knowledge, Spells, Engine** — the raw event stream, the item / mob / quest data, the
  spell database, and the engine's health.

## Around the window

- **Search** (top centre) finds zones, mobs and items from anywhere: a zone or mob opens it on the
  map, an item opens its card.
- **The character picker** (top right) switches between the characters whose logs the app found.
- **Log live** shows the app is following the game's log as it is written.
- **DPS overlay** opens a floating damage meter above the game.
- **Preferences** (⌘,) — where the game is installed, your class loadout, the window and overlays,
  the combat meters, voice packs, the cursor ring, and what's new in each release.
